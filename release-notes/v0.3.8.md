# StreamDek TV v0.3.8

Stronger adult-content protection across every source, a cleaner player control bar, and more
dependable playback details for add-on titles.

## What's New

**Stronger adult-content protection**
When adult-content protection is on, explicit repositories, plugins, providers, live channels and
titles are now kept out of Home, Fuse, Search, Live, your Library and the player, whichever
source or add-on they arrive through. Protection now recognises sources that try to slip through
with renamed titles, extra punctuation, look-alike letters or joined-up names, and checks live
channels by their playlist identity as well as their display name.

**Protection updates reach the screen straight away**
When StreamDek updates its protection rules, your TV applies them within a few minutes, and
anything newly restricted disappears from Home, Fuse, Search, Live and your Library without
restarting the app. Rows that would be left empty are removed rather than shown bare.

**Refreshed player controls**
The player's control bar now uses larger, rounded icons without the circular backgrounds. The
highlighted control turns gold and lifts clearly above its neighbours, so it is easy to see from
across the room. Options that are switched on stay bright white with a small gold marker.

## Improvements

**The source you are watching comes first**
Opening **Sources** in the player now starts on the source that is currently playing, followed
by your favourite sources.

**Better details for add-on titles**
Films, series and live channels from add-ons are now recognised more reliably, so their details,
artwork and episode information are found more often and live channels are no longer mistaken
for series.

**Protection that stays on offline**
Your TV remembers the most recent protection rules. If StreamDek cannot be reached, those rules
stay in force and blocking stays switched on. A rule update that cannot be verified as coming
from StreamDek is ignored.

**Translated messages**
The new playback message for restricted content is available in all supported app languages.

## Fixed

- Fixed restricted titles sometimes remaining visible in Home, Fuse and search results until the
  app was restarted after protection rules changed.
- Fixed adult plugins inside large "all-in-one" collections, and previously enabled adult
  plugins restored from settings, being loaded when protection was on.
- Fixed playback of a restricted title starting from Continue Watching, a remembered source or a
  direct link. It is now stopped before the video starts.
- Fixed live channels with a harmless display name bypassing protection when their playlist
  identity matched a restricted source.
- Fixed saved items with missing optional details causing errors when they were loaded.
- Fixed occasional failures when the connection dropped while a response was still arriving.
- Fixed a series page left open behind the home screen continuing to check for watched-episode
  updates every few seconds until the app was closed. It now checks only while StreamDek is on
  screen, and catches up as soon as you return.

## Quick Setup

- **Nothing to set up:** Protection follows your StreamDek account settings and updates
  automatically.
