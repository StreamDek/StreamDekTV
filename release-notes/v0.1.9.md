# StreamDek TV v0.1.9

## What's New

- Added a brightness menu to the player, so the picture can be taken down for a dark room. Full brightness is the default and leaves the picture untouched; the chosen level is remembered on this TV.
- Added a "Hide home synopsis" setting under Library & Home. With it on, the Home spotlight drops the description and centres the title and its details in the band.
- Added a kind badge above the Home spotlight title — Movie, Series, Documentary, Reality, Talk Show, News — and live channels show their source there.
- Added a "Transparent navigation" setting under Appearance, letting the backdrop show through the navigation rail. On by default, and capped so the rail always reads as a surface.
- Added a More button to the synopsis in Media Details, opening the full description over the page.
- Episodes now run continuously across seasons. Reaching the end of a season loads the next one and carries straight on into it, with the season pills following the episode you are on.

## Improvements

- Skip Intro, Skip Recap and Next Episode prompts now hold the remote while they are on screen. Nothing else can be summoned until the prompt is taken or dismissed with Back, so a single press can no longer half-open the transport controls behind it.
- The Home screen shows one complete row and a third of the next, giving the spotlight room to breathe instead of leaving the screen bottom-heavy. The proportion now adapts to card shape and density rather than assuming one layout.
- Home content sits closer to the navigation rail, which no longer leaves a channel of dead space down the left.
- The Home spotlight holds still as focus moves between cards. The badge, title and metadata each keep a fixed place, so only their contents change.
- Reduced the dark wash over Home artwork, particularly the top right of the frame, which is the part with nothing over it.
- Selecting a stream now goes straight to the player instead of rebuilding the stream list on the way out.
- The player opens on the title's own artwork immediately rather than holding on black while it fetches details it already had.
- Added space between the ratings and genre row and the synopsis in the Home spotlight.
- The on-screen clock carries a subtle shadow so it stays legible over bright artwork.

## Fixes

- Fixed a crash that closed the app when pressing up on Home. Continue Watching can hold the same title twice, and the duplicate was fatal as soon as focus searched past the visible cards.
- Fixed the season pills in Media Details being impossible to focus. Anything arriving in the episodes section was redirected straight to an episode card, so the D-pad went round the pills every time.
- Fixed being unable to get back up to the hero from the episode row. The way out is now explicit: an episode card leads to the season pills, and the pills lead to Play.
- Fixed a collapsed Home row stopping short of the screen edge and appearing to be missing its last card.
- Fixed the next-episode dialog opening without taking focus, which let the player controls be summoned behind it.
- Fixed the navigation highlight taking on whatever artwork sat behind it once the rail became transparent.
