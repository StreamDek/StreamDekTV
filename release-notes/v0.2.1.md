# StreamDek TV v0.2.1

## What's New

- IPTV playlists now play on the TV. A playlist added in StreamDek Mobile or the web portal appears on Live TV as its own source, with its channels grouped into the categories the playlist itself declares. The playlist is fetched and read here on the TV, and a copy is kept, so the channels are on screen straight away next time and still work when the provider cannot be reached. Add and remove playlists from the phone or the portal — a provider address is a long line of credentials and no one wants to type it on a remote — and turn them on and off under Settings → Sources.
- Live TV opens as a list. A channel lineup runs to thousands of entries and the name is what you scan for, so the list is what you get and artwork is one press away: Card view sits near the top of the sidebar, under Favourites. Card view now uses proper portrait channel artwork rather than stretched thumbnails.
- The Live TV sidebar lists categories. Each source's own categories appear rather than one entry per source, with the source named underneath so two providers that both offer "Sport" stay apart. Prefer the old flat list? Turn categories off under Settings → Live TV.
- Favourite the channel you are watching, from the player. A star sits in the player controls between Sources and Brightness. Until now a channel could only be favourited by holding OK on it in the grid, which is no use once you are already watching it.
- Search now asks your add-ons as well. It searched TMDB and the live channels it had already loaded; every add-on catalogue that supports searching is now asked the same question, so a title only an add-on carries can actually be found. Those matches join the results as they arrive rather than holding up the first ones.
- Settings has been rebuilt around what each setting affects: Playback, Skip & Autoplay, Streams & Quality, Home & Layout, Live TV, Appearance, Accessibility, Sources, Connections, About — ordered by how often they are reached for. Nothing was dropped, and searching still finds the old names: "providers" reaches Sources, "diagnostics" reaches About, "tracking" and "devices" both reach Connections.
- A Live TV page in Settings gathers channel cards, categories, the player's favourites drawer and the live progress bar in one place, matching the phone.

## Improvements

- Loading a playlist now says what it is doing. A large provider list is tens of megabytes and can hold hundreds of thousands of channels, and the Live page used to sit there saying nothing while it worked. It now reports connecting, then reading with a running channel count and a progress bar, then sorting the channels into categories — which is the stage where it used to look most stuck.
- Channel card style, categories and the favourites drawer setting now follow what you chose on the phone. Those choices were already syncing to the TV; nothing here was reading them.
- The Home spotlight no longer shows the synopsis by default. At sofa distance the paragraph is rarely read and costs the artwork the room it earns. Turn it back on under Settings → Home & Layout.
- Settings → Sources lists your playlists with their current state, and Refresh pulls them alongside add-ons, plugins and premium services.

## Fixes

- Fixed the app closing when you pressed left or right on the Live TV page. Moving out of the channel grid after scrolling the sidebar, or moving right while the page was still loading or a filter had emptied it, could take the app down.
- Fixed the same crash on Search and on Browse all, where a search that matched nothing left the filter row with nowhere to move down to.
- Fixed add-on catalogues with a space in their id loading the wrong list, or the same list as every other catalogue, when StreamDek fetched them directly.
