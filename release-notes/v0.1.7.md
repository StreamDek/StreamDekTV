# StreamDek TV v0.1.7

## What's New

- Added an icon-based card/list switcher in the Live TV favourites drawer, matching the mobile app.

## Improvements

- Reduced the favourites drawer's card size and replaced the thick focus border with a slimmer, modern highlight.
- Resized Library's cards to match Search's density and rebalanced their proportions.
- The Library hero image and details now update as focus moves between cards, instead of staying stuck on the first one.
- Strengthened heading and section-label contrast across Search, Live, and Library so they stay readable over backdrop art.
- The floating top navigation now stays on the current page when moving focus up from content, instead of snapping back to Home.

## Fixes

- Fixed a crash when removing a favourite channel from the player.
- Fixed a crash when selecting certain saved channels from the Favourites drawer.
- Fixed the Favourites drawer's card/list view switcher not responding to clicks.
- Fixed scrolling within the Favourites drawer unexpectedly opening the channel row underneath.
- Fixed selecting the already-playing favourite leaving the Favourites drawer stuck open.
- Fixed a crash on launch that could occur while regenerating a dangling TV handoff key.
