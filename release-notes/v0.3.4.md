# StreamDek TV v0.3.4

## What's New

- **Minimum required version.** StreamDek can now tell a version that is no longer
  supported to update before it continues. The update screen shows the installed and required
  versions with **Update now** and **Retry**. When a release is available through StreamDek's
  built-in updater, **Update now** installs it directly; otherwise it opens the update page. Back
  cannot bypass the screen, and if the version check cannot be reached, StreamDek opens normally
  rather than blocking you.

- **An update screen that matches your StreamDek.** The screen shows the StreamDek logo and uses
  your theme's colours, including High contrast. The focused button takes your theme's accent, so
  the remote's position is clear from across the room. StreamDek remembers the last theme this
  television showed, so the screen matches even before your profile has loaded.

- **Better support for CloudStream sources.** Extensions that expect CloudStream's activity context,
  including CNC Verse providers, can now load through a compatible host instead of failing while
  they are enabled.

- **CloudStream rows on Home.** CloudStream sources that offer their own main page — trending lists,
  studios, live events — can put those rows on Home. Switch them on in **Home rows**, where they sit
  under the plugin that provides them, with the collection it came from (for example
  "CloudStream · CNC Repo") on its own line beneath. They start switched off, only sources that are
  switched on are listed, and Home updates as soon as a source is turned on or off. A row arranged
  on the phone is the same row here.

- **CloudStream titles open and play.** A title opened from a CloudStream row shows its details,
  seasons and episodes, and plays sources from the provider that listed it. When the provider knows
  the title's TMDB or IMDb id, you get the full StreamDek page, and that provider's sources still
  appear alongside your add-ons.

- **Know where a source comes from.** Streams from CloudStream collections are labelled
  "CloudStream · <collection>" in the sources list and the player, as on mobile.

- **Trailers in the quality you asked for.** Trailers now play at the resolution set in
  **Settings → Trailer quality**, up to 4K where the trailer offers it. Until now nearly every
  trailer arrived at 360p whatever that setting said: the service StreamDek fetched them from would
  only serve the opening few megabytes of a file, so the only version that could be played from
  beginning to end was the smallest one. Across a television that difference is the whole picture.
  StreamDek now asks in a way that gets the whole file, so a two-minute trailer at 1080p — around
  50 MB — plays in full rather than being ruled out before it starts.

- **Trailers play to the end.** A trailer that stopped part way through, or cut out mid-scene and
  returned to the page, was running out of the portion StreamDek was allowed to fetch. That limit
  no longer applies, and trailers run to their last frame.

- **Trailers in the film's own language.** Studios upload a trailer with its dubbed versions
  attached — eight languages is ordinary — and StreamDek could pick any one of them, because it
  chose the version encoded at the highest quality rather than the one the film was made in. On one
  trailer the German dub was a fraction ahead of the English original, so the English film played
  in German. StreamDek now uses the original language track that the upload itself identifies.

- **More trailer links simply work.** Trailers arrive from catalogues and add-ons in whatever form
  each one uses. Short links, embedded players, Shorts, premiere and live pages, privacy-mode links
  and plain video ids are now all understood. Links StreamDek could not read before were treated as
  "no trailer at all", even when the video behind them was fine.

- **Streaming network pages keep loading.** Browsing a streaming network's titles now loads the next
  page as you reach the end, and keeps loading while there is more to show. Titles that appear on
  more than one page are shown once, and searching within a network pages the same way.

- **Play, Resume and Play again say the right thing.** A film's main button now follows where you
  actually are in it. Starting a film you have watched before shows **Resume** while you are part
  way through, rather than still offering **Play again** from an earlier viewing, and returns to
  **Play again** once you reach the end. Marking a film unwatched returns it to **Play**.

## Improvements

- **The right trailer, chosen more often.** StreamDek now reads what the catalogue says each video
  is — trailer, teaser, clip, featurette, whether the studio published it and when — instead of
  working it out afterwards by fetching each one. In the weeks around a release, when a title's
  videos are mostly ticket adverts and "Now Playing" stings, the actual trailer is picked out
  before anything is downloaded. For a series, the show's own trailer is preferred to a single
  season's teaser.
- **Trailers recover instead of failing.** Trailer links are issued against one of several servers,
  and a server that refuses meant no trailer at all. StreamDek now checks the link works before
  playing and moves to another server when it does not.
- **A trailer that cannot be found stays out of the way.** When no trailer can be played, the
  detail page carries on exactly as it is, with the artwork in place. Nothing about the failure is
  put in front of you.
- **Trailers stay inside StreamDek.** Nothing about trailer playback opens a browser or another app.
- **Sturdier CloudStream support in release builds.** The names CloudStream extensions are loaded by
  are kept intact in optimised builds, alongside the rest of the API they call into.
- **More translated screens.** The update screen, including "Preparing update…" and its fallback
  title and message, and the Restart Episode and Restart Movie actions are now translated into
  German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Polish and Portuguese.
- **Artwork for add-on and plugin titles.** Titles from add-ons and plugins that bring only a poster
  used to open on a page with no artwork behind it. The poster now sits whole, as a rounded card to
  the right of the reading column, over a softly blurred copy of itself that fills the screen with
  its colours. Artwork that already suits the screen is shown as before, and catalogue titles are
  unchanged.

## Fixed

- Fixed the update screen using fixed colours that ignored the selected theme.
- Fixed CloudStream extensions that cast their host to `AppCompatActivity` failing to enable.
- Fixed trailers playing at 360p regardless of the trailer quality setting.
- Fixed trailers stopping part way through and returning to the page.
- Fixed a trailer occasionally playing in a dubbed language rather than the film's own.
- Fixed trailer links in Shorts, premiere, live and privacy-mode forms being treated as though the
  title had no trailer.
- Fixed a trailer failing to start when the server its link was issued against would not answer.
- Fixed streaming network pages stopping after the first page of titles, and the same title
  appearing more than once while browsing.
- Fixed a film you were part way through offering **Play again** instead of **Resume**.
