# StreamDek TV v0.3.6

## What's New

**Your audio and subtitle choices survive a compatibility switch**
When a title hands over to the compatibility player part-way through, the audio track and
subtitles you chose are carried across, matched by language and title rather than by the position
they happened to hold. Subtitles you switched off stay off, and a match the app cannot make
confidently is left alone rather than guessed at.

## Improvements

**Dolby Vision Profile 7**
Profile 7 titles now keep native Dolby Vision when the television and its display actually support
it, and switch to compatible HEVC playback only when they do not, or when decoding fails. The
switch happens at most once per source and no longer treats missing profile information as Profile
7, so ordinary HEVC and the other Dolby Vision profiles are left alone. Protected titles keep their
existing playback path. **DV7 - HEVC Fallback** is now on by default; a choice you have already
made is kept.

**A tidier player information line**
The source beneath the title now has a defined width, with clear space kept between it and the
**Ends at** time. A source name too long for that space scrolls gently within it rather than
running towards the end time.

**Lighter on memory, and lets go when you leave**
The player now sizes its buffer for a television rather than for a desktop, and releases it
altogether when you switch to another app or the box goes to standby - picking the title back up
where you left it when you return. Your position is saved at that moment too, so a box that
reclaims memory while StreamDek is in the background no longer costs you the last few minutes.

**Less work on the way up**
StreamDek now ships a startup profile, so the code that runs while the app opens is prepared ahead
of time instead of on the day. Launch is a little quicker and frame times through the opening are
steadier, most noticeably on the first launch after an update.

## Fixed

- Fixed **Remembered source expired** appearing minutes into an episode that was playing perfectly
  well. A source that has already put a picture on screen is no longer read as an expired link, so
  reopening it - for a compatibility switch, or after a brief interruption - no longer interrupts
  the episode or throws away a stored source that was working.
- Fixed a brief interruption costing you the source you were watching. A source that has been
  playing is now reopened at your position first, and the list is only walked if that fails.
- Fixed the automatic switch to the compatibility player firing for a title that had been playing
  for some time, where the fault was the connection rather than the format.
- Fixed the player continuing to hold its buffers, and to tick, after the television had moved on
  to something else.
