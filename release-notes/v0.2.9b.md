# StreamDek TV v0.2.9b

## What's New

- **DNS over HTTPS is available in TV settings.** Choose Cloudflare, Google, AdGuard, Quad9,
  DNS.SB, Canadian Shield or a validated custom HTTPS endpoint. Each provider's address is shown
  beneath its name in the provider selector, with a clearer explanation of which StreamDek
  requests the setting covers.

## Improvements

- **Next Episode finds sources progressively and fails safely.** Results appear as providers
  finish, discovery and resolution have bounded timeouts, stale searches are cancelled, and a
  failed automatic choice returns to the available source options instead of leaving the viewer
  on an endless loading screen.

- **Episode identity is checked before automatic playback.** Next Episode keeps the canonical
  series identity together with season and episode numbers, preventing similarly named shows
  from being selected merely because their episode numbers match.

- **The player now has separate seek and controls focus areas.** Left and Right remain owned by
  the progress row for continuous scrubbing; Down intentionally enters the player controls and
  Up returns to the progress row. The seek highlight no longer escapes into Sources, Subtitles or
  another action during repeated remote input.

- **TV side navigation is predictable.** One Left press from the first Home card opens the menu,
  Up and Down keep it open while moving between destinations, and Right, Back or selecting a
  destination closes it with focus restored to visible content. Returning Home no longer carries
  stale drawer state from another page.

- **Home focus follows the content that is actually available.** Launching the app, completing
  sign-in or leaving the side navigation now focuses the first card in the first non-empty Home
  row. Continue Watching is preferred when present but is no longer required for navigation to
  work.

- **Search has a direct route back to navigation.** Left from the highlighted search field opens
  the side menu when the field is not being edited, while active text editing retains normal
  cursor behaviour. The former Profile navigation label is now the clearer **Settings** label.

- **Stream results are easier to scan.** Ready-provider badges remain at the top-right of source
  cards, and source resolution avoids repeatedly walking through multiple failing torrent
  results before returning control to the viewer.

- **Plugin Stream results are now fully returned.** Plugin sources enabled who have stream results 
  now all show up in the streams list page matching what showed up in the sources drawer of the 
  player screens. Note: a source that has no result will not show up.

## Fixed

- Fixed the synopsis for the completed episode remaining visible while StreamDek searches for
  the next episode. The next-episode card remains visible, while the old synopsis panel is hidden
  during source discovery.

- Fixed removing one Continue Watching item temporarily clearing the entire Library view. Only
  the selected card is removed, the remaining collection stays visible, and focus moves to a
  valid neighbouring card.

- Fixed subtitle drawer section headings sitting outside the list's intended horizontal padding.

- Fixed rapid side-menu navigation collapsing the drawer after every focus movement, requiring
  a second Left press to open it, or leaving focus attached to an invisible collapsed item.

- Fixed horizontal player seeking unexpectedly transferring focus into the controls row.
