# StreamDek TV v0.3.9

Bring your Plex and Jellyfin libraries into StreamDek, browse a series' episodes without
leaving the player, try the new libVLC player engine, and open to a Home screen that loads once
and stays put.

## What's New

**Plex on your TV**
Connect your Plex account to browse and play your own films and series inside StreamDek.
Choose which servers and libraries appear, including libraries shared with you. Your titles
join Home and Search, with a dedicated Plex page for browsing libraries, recently added and
recently watched titles, and collections. Title pages also show reviews supplied by Plex
where available.

**Jellyfin support**
Connect your own Jellyfin server using Quick Connect or your username and password. StreamDek
can find servers on your home network, or you can enter an address yourself. Add more than
one server and choose the libraries you want to see. Browse films, series, collections,
Favourites and Next Up alongside your recently added titles.

**One place for your media servers**
When both services are enabled, **My Media** appears beside Search in the side menu, with
separate **Plex** and **Jellyfin** tabs. With one service enabled, the menu uses that service's
name. Open a library to browse by **Recently added**, **A–Z** or **Release date**. Shelves load
more titles as you move along them, and returning from a title restores your place.

**See what has loaded ahead**
The player's timeline now shows buffered video in a lighter shade of your chosen theme colour,
alongside the portion you have already watched. This works with ExoPlayer and both mpv display
modes. The round marker has been removed from the bar so nothing covers what has loaded; the bar
thickens when you move onto it, and the time preview follows your theme.

**Episodes in the player**
While watching a series, open **Episodes** in the player's control bar to browse every season
without going back to the title page. The episode you are watching is marked, watched and
part-watched episodes show their progress, and episodes that have not aired yet are labelled.
Choose one to switch to it straight away.

**libVLC player engine**
libVLC joins ExoPlayer and mpv as a third player engine. Choose it under **Settings → Player →
Default player**, or switch to it during playback from the player's **Engine** panel without
losing your place. **Auto** works as before. If libVLC cannot play a source, StreamDek hands it
to another engine and tells you; sources that need sign-in headers or are protected go straight
to ExoPlayer.

**A new look for icons**
The side menu, the player and Settings now share one set of StreamDek icons, matching the
mobile app. Menu icons fill in when highlighted. Plex and Jellyfin keep their own marks.

**A background for StreamDek Fuse**
The Fuse page now has its own colour wash, led by your chosen accent colour, like the Plex and
Jellyfin pages.

## Improvements

**Home loads once**
Opening StreamDek no longer shows Home appearing, reloading and reloading again. The screen is
now revealed once and stays in place while Continue Watching, your media servers and add-on rows
fill in, and the highlight is not pulled back to the first card. If you have used StreamDek on
this TV before, Home opens on the page you last saw and then updates quietly. Switching profile
no longer shows the previous profile's rows for a moment.

**Tidier Plex and Jellyfin settings**
Each server is now a group you can fold away, showing how many of its libraries are on. Remove
a single library, or a single server, without disconnecting everything else: press Right on a
library to reach its remove button, or choose **Remove this server**. Removed Plex servers and
libraries are kept under **Removed** so you can restore them. Nothing is deleted on the server
itself.

**More in the player's info panel**
The info panel now shows more where the engine reports it, including video profile and bit
depth, audio sample rate, bitrate and language, the current stream bitrate, and frames decoded
and dropped.

**Continue watching your own library**
In-progress Plex and Jellyfin titles join StreamDek's Continue Watching. When matching titles
appear in both places, the most recently watched entry supplies the resume position and source.
Playback progress and watched changes are also reported back to the relevant media server.
With a media service enabled, your Library shows Continue Watching and Watchlist together on
one page, with shortcuts to jump between them.

**Your server setup follows your profile**
Plex and Jellyfin connections and library choices sync through your StreamDek profile, so
compatible devices using the same profile can pick them up. Returning to Home refreshes the
server setup, including connections made on another device.

**More control over personal media**
Your media-server rows can be shown, hidden and reordered with the other Home rows. Plex
playback offers Direct Play, Direct Stream and server conversion options according to the
video and your TV's capabilities. A **Remote streaming quality** setting lets you limit the
quality requested away from your Plex server's network. Both services have an optional
**Ambient colour** setting for their browsing pages.

**Settings that survive a restart**
StreamDek now keeps a local copy of account and profile settings and remembers pending changes
while offline. It restores those settings when you return and retries cloud saves when the
connection is available. Delayed responses are prevented from replacing newer local edits,
and each account keeps its own remembered profile.

**Clearer server status and recovery**
Media pages explain when a server is asleep, unreachable or refusing access. Failed library
loads offer **Try again** with more detail about the problem. Library requests have more time
to finish, and Jellyfin loading is coordinated to reduce repeated requests.

**Translated setup and controls**
The new media-server setup screens, browsing controls and settings messages are available in
all supported app languages.

## Fixed

- Fixed Home rebuilding and resetting the highlight several times after a fresh launch.
- Fixed settings falling back to defaults after an offline start or failed settings refresh.
- Fixed pending settings changes being lost across restarts or mixed up during account and
  profile changes.
- Fixed audio and subtitle language choices in Settings not consistently matching the
  preferences used for source selection and playback.
- Fixed the live progress-bar option not following the active profile's playback preference.
- Added a clear message when a setting cannot be saved to the TV's storage.

## Quick Setup

- **Connect Plex:** Sign in to StreamDek, then open **Settings → Plex → Connect Plex**.
  Scan the QR code or follow the displayed linking instructions on your phone or computer.
  Choose the servers and libraries you want to show.
- **Connect Jellyfin:** Open **Settings → Jellyfin**. Find your server on the network or enter
  its address, then use **Quick Connect** or sign in with your Jellyfin username and password.
  Enable the libraries you want to browse.
- **Find your libraries:** Open **Plex**, **Jellyfin** or **My Media** beside Search in the side
  menu. The entry appears when a connected service has a library enabled.
- **Remove a library or server:** Open **Settings → Plex** or **Settings → Jellyfin**, open the
  server, then press Right on a library and choose remove, or select **Remove this server**.
  Restore anything from the **Removed** section.
- **Browse episodes while watching:** Bring up the player's controls and choose **Episodes**.
- **Try libVLC:** Open **Settings → Player → Default player** and choose **libVLC**, or open
  **Engine** in the player to switch for the title you are watching.
- **Arrange Home:** Open **Settings → Home Screen → Home rows** to choose and order your
  media-server rows.
- **Adjust Plex streaming quality:** Open **Settings → Plex → Remote streaming quality**.
- **Change the browsing background:** Open **Settings → Plex → Ambient colour** or
  **Settings → Jellyfin → Ambient colour**.

## Good to Know

- libVLC cannot send custom sign-in headers or play protected (ClearKey) channels, does not show
  loaded-ahead video on the timeline, and applies subtitle size and position changes by briefly
  reopening the video at the same position. Dolby Vision plays as HDR10 where the file allows.
- Removing a library or a Plex server hides it on this TV; it is switched off for your profile
  on every device.
