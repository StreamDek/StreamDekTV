# StreamDek TV v0.1.4

## What's New

- Added secure playback handoff from StreamDek Mobile, preserving the selected source, quality, episode, and playback position without storing readable stream URLs on the server.
- Added profile-scoped Live TV favourites that sync between mobile and TV.
- Added a polished favourites drawer inside the Live TV player, including cross-source channel switching and long-press removal.
- Redesigned Library with a cinematic focused-title hero, metadata, synopsis, poster shelves, and Movies/Series filters.
- Redesigned Search and Live TV with fluid, remote-friendly filter rails and poster/channel grids.

## Improvements

- Handoff requests now expire after two minutes and are acknowledged and removed after delivery.
- Improved device identity reconciliation to prevent duplicate TV sessions after reinstalling or reconnecting.
- Improved automatic Media3-to-MPV fallback and clearer playback recovery behavior.
- Improved in-app update validation and installer handling for Fire TV and Android TV devices.
- Live favourites remain available from the local cache when the TV is temporarily offline and reconcile after the account reconnects.

## Privacy and Fixes

- Handoff payloads are end-to-end encrypted for the selected TV and cannot be read as plain content URLs in the StreamDek database.
- Synced favourite records exclude playable stream URLs and request headers.
- Fixed the player favourites drawer being limited to the currently playing add-on.
- Fixed stale and duplicate device sessions caused by app reinstallations or regenerated local identifiers.
