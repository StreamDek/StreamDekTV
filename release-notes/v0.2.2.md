# StreamDek TV v0.2.2

## What's New

- Trailers now play directly inside the title page with a TV-sized, aspect-correct presentation. Settings → Home & Layout includes the same 360p, 720p, 1080p and 2160p trailer-quality choices as mobile, and the trailer engine honours that maximum when selecting and playing media.
- Search now has a dedicated Voice search button. Speak a title with the TV remote and the recognised phrase is placed straight into StreamDek search.
- The TV now opens with a profile picker. Profiles with a PIN ask for it before switching, and Settings → Account includes “Remember Last profile at startup” for households that prefer to go directly to Home.
- Usenet sources can now be assembled and played on the TV. If a source cannot be resolved or playback fails, StreamDek works through the remaining ranked sources and explains what it is trying on the loading screen.
- Premium-service setup on TV now supports the full provider list. Device-code providers use their television sign-in flow, typed keys are validated before saving, and keys can remain on the television when local-only storage is selected.
- Add-on and plugin results arrive progressively instead of waiting for every provider. Results that finish early remain visible while slower providers continue.

## Improvements

- The Sources settings page puts premium services first, identifies whether each source is a plugin or an add-on, collapses long collections, and keeps Refresh sources near the page heading.
- The title-page trailer resolver considers all available videos and their running times, preferring a full trailer over short teasers, ticket spots and notices.
- The navigation rail is transparent while collapsed, expands from the edge when focused, and is available from title pages as well as the main destinations.
- Home hero movement, settings panels and player controls have been refined for smoother TV focus and motion. Subtitle adjustment now has its own player tab, and Stream info can scroll independently.
- Search-only add-on catalogues are no longer requested as empty Home rows.

## Fixes

- Fixed trailers being enlarged or stretched. Wide and older-format videos now letterbox or pillarbox without cropping.
- Fixed the startup profile picker losing focus to the Home screen behind it. The picker owns the screen until a profile is selected, and profile names and status labels remain readable at TV distance.
- Fixed the navigation rail opening automatically after entering Search or returning to Home. Focus now transfers to the destination content and Home restores the previously highlighted card after the route transition.
- Fixed progressive provider batches replacing results that had already arrived.
- Fixed several player fallback paths silently waiting or stopping after the first failed source.
