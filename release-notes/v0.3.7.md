# StreamDek TV v0.3.7

Get sound and subtitles back in sync with easier remote controls, a wider subtitle adjustment
range, and clearer playback settings.

## What's New

**Adjust sound to match the picture**
If dialogue arrives before or after someone's lips move, open **Audio** in the player and use
**Audio delay** to bring it back into sync. Move sound up to **5 seconds earlier or later**.
Tap left or right on your remote for small, 0.05-second adjustments, or hold the button for larger,
0.5-second steps. **Reset audio delay** returns the current video's adjustment to zero.

An adjustment made in the player applies to the current video. Each new video starts from your
TV's saved default.

**Save an audio adjustment for your TV setup**
If your soundbar, receiver or headphones regularly need the same correction, set it once under
**Settings → Audio → Default audio delay**. The adjustment is remembered on this TV only, so it
will not change the timing on your phone or another television. It starts at zero, and you can
use **Reset audio delay** on that settings page to return the default to zero.

**Dedicated Audio and Subtitles settings pages**
Audio and subtitle preferences now have their own pages in Settings, under the Playback category.
Your spoken-language preference is in **Audio**. Subtitle language, secondary language, automatic
loading, language filtering and source choices are in **Subtitles**, with guidance on adjusting
timing while watching. Your existing preferences are kept.

## Improvements

**A much wider subtitle timing range**
Subtitle delay now reaches **2 minutes earlier or later**, up from 15 seconds. Open
**Subtitles → Adjust** in the player to correct subtitles that appear too soon or too late.
The change applies to the current video and resets for the next one.

**Fine adjustments without endless button presses**
Subtitle timing now moves in **0.1-second steps** with a tap. Hold left or right to move in
1-second steps, then 10-second steps as you keep holding. A new **Reset subtitle delay** button
restores the original timing immediately.

**Clearer timing explanations**
Audio and subtitle controls explain whether your adjustment moves sound or text earlier or later.
The on-screen hints also explain how to use the remote and which adjustments are saved.

**Audio sync support across both players**
Audio adjustment is available in ExoPlayer and mpv, including both mpv display modes. When
ExoPlayer is using **Tunneled Playback**, the audio panel explains why an adjustment cannot take
effect and how to use another playback option. Settings also shows a reminder when tunnelling
is enabled.

**Easier settings search**
Search terms such as **lip sync**, **audio delay** and **subtitle timing** help you find the relevant
settings pages. Player settings now focuses on the player, display, sleep and compatibility options.

**Translated controls and guidance**
The new settings, timing controls and help text are available in all supported app languages.

## Fixed

- Fixed subtitle delay having no effect on embedded subtitles and broadcast captions in
  ExoPlayer. Adjustments now apply to those tracks as well as separately loaded subtitle files.
- Fixed mpv ignoring a subtitle timing adjustment sent before the player was ready, including
  during a switch between playback engines. Timing is now applied once the player is ready in
  both mpv display modes, and the new audio adjustment is handled the same way.

## Quick Setup

- **Fix sound for one video:** Open **Audio** in the player, highlight **Audio delay**, then press
  left to move sound earlier or right to move it later. If voices arrive late, move sound earlier;
  if they arrive too soon, move it later.
- **Set a correction for your sound system:** Open **Settings → Audio → Default audio delay** and
  adjust with left or right. Hold for larger steps. Use up or down to leave the adjustment row.
  Leave the default at zero unless your setup needs a correction.
- **Fix subtitle timing:** Open **Subtitles → Adjust** in the player and highlight **Subtitle
  delay**. Press left if subtitles appear late, or right if they appear too early. Hold the button
  for larger changes. For embedded subtitles and live captions, how far they can appear early
  depends on how much the stream has loaded ahead.
- **If audio delay is unavailable:** Turn off **Tunneled Playback** under **Settings → Streams and
  Quality**, or switch the current video to **mpv** from the player's engine panel.
