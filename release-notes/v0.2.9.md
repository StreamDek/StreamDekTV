# StreamDek TV v0.2.9

## What's New

- **Continue Watching now follows the exact movie or episode across devices.** A TV resume no
  longer depends on a temporary stream link from the phone. StreamDek carries the title, season,
  episode and position into the TV's normal source search, then applies the resume only after the
  selected media is ready. Continue Watching and New Episodes cards now show the season and
  episode they will open.

- **Series pages open where you actually are.** A partly watched episode is selected with its
  saved position; after a completed episode, the next unwatched released episode is selected.
  Watched state is no longer inferred from whether the series happens to be in the Home Continue
  Watching row.

- **Episode and season watched actions are reversible.** The same action changes between Mark as
  Watched and Mark as Unwatched, updates the page immediately, and synchronizes the exact episode
  state through SyncDek and configured watch providers.

## Improvements

- **Subtitle source preferences now control the results, not just the opening tab.** Choose All
  sources, Built-in or Add-ons. The player labels every result by its real origin, keeps built-in
  and add-on results in their proper lists, and gives each source view a useful empty state.

- **Preferred subtitle languages are enforced.** With Show Only Preferred Languages enabled,
  only the primary and optional secondary language are shown. Language names, two-letter codes,
  three-letter codes and regional variants are normalized before filtering, and the drawer says
  which requested languages had no results.

- **Source rows are easier to scan.** The Real-Debrid Ready badge stays at the true top-right of
  the card, release names no longer leak into the quality column, and StreamDek prefers the actual
  movie-sized value when an add-on also reports a small response or chunk size.

- **Only released seasons are selectable.** Empty and future seasons no longer appear simply
  because a metadata provider has created a placeholder for them.

- **Profile changes return to a predictable Home screen.** Selecting a profile always lands on
  Home with focus reset to the first card in the top row, including when the previous profile was
  changed from Settings.

## Fixed

- Fixed TV Continue Watching losing an episode's season/episode identity, attempting playback as a
  series, or showing `The coroutine scope left the composition` while the exact episode request
  replaced the initial request.

- Fixed stale resume work carrying one episode's timestamp into the next. A new episode starts at
  zero unless that exact episode already has legitimate progress, and the seek is applied only
  after the loaded media is ready.

- Fixed Next Episode leaving the episode just completed unwatched when the transition was entered
  from the legitimate ending/outro region.

- Fixed season-wide watched updates reaching SyncDek while episode cards and season tabs remained
  visually stale. Episode identities are normalized consistently and the detail page refreshes
  the aggregate season state while it is open.

- Fixed older SyncDek servers returning one exact progress record to a request that expected the
  full series history. TV now uses the list contract with a backward-compatible filtered fallback,
  so completed seasons and cross-device episode progress are reconstructed correctly.

- Fixed built-in OpenSubtitles results being classified as add-on results, source preferences
  merely changing the active drawer tab, and non-preferred languages remaining visible after the
  preferred-language filter was enabled.
