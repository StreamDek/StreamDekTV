# StreamDek TV v0.1.5

## What's New

- Redesigned Search, Live TV, and Library around a consistent compact navigation rail and five-column portrait-card layout.
- Added a refined Live TV favourites drawer with a compact text list by default and an optional artwork-card view.
- Added live-channel history so Back returns to the previously watched channel before leaving the player.

## Improvements

- Made navigation rails transparent, higher, compact, scrollable, and consistent with Settings.
- Improved text, focus, and surface contrast across search filters, Live TV, Library, and content grids.
- Improved Live TV View All filters and aligned content positioning across TV pages.
- Limited the Channels and Favourites player hints to three seconds every fifteen seconds.
- Sized the favourites drawer to one third of the player with a soft faded edge and right-aligned content.

## Fixes

- Restored secure Mobile-to-TV handoff prompts with compatible RSA-OAEP decryption and refreshed device-key registration.
- Fixed Live TV add-on results being omitted from Search.
- Fixed long filter lists becoming unreachable with a TV remote.
- Fixed Back exiting playback immediately after switching Live TV channels.
