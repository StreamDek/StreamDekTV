# StreamDek TV v0.3.5

## What's New

- **StreamDek Fuse.** Bring Live TV and on-demand titles from your add-ons, CloudStream sources
  and playlists into one destination. Enable Fuse in Settings to replace the source rows on Home
  with a single entry, with a shortcut in the navigation rail. Browse by source, collection or
  category, switch between live and on-demand content, and search across your sources. Sources
  without catalogue search search only their loaded titles. Fuse is off by default and its toggle
  is saved on this device.

- **Next Up in Continue Watching.** Finishing an episode now makes room for the next episode once
  it has aired, including the next season when available. An unfinished episode keeps its resume
  position, and a newly aired episode can bring a series back towards the front of the row.

- **CloudStream settings on the television.** Open supported extensions' own settings from
  StreamDek, search their lists and change the sources they offer with the remote. Changes apply
  when you close the settings. Source switches now follow your profile between Mobile and TV,
  with newer changes preserved when devices reconnect.

## Improvements

- **Trailers grow out of the hero.** The picture gently fades in behind the title, synopsis,
  metadata and actions. The hero remains readable for four seconds after the first frame, then
  its content and shading fade away over 1.4 seconds. Ending or stopping the trailer dissolves
  back to the artwork and hero content. The transition uses opacity without moving the layout.
- **Live channels from CloudStream.** Provider channels are available in the player's channel list.
  Channels with several sources can try another source when the first fails to start, with clear
  feedback while connecting and a message when none responds.
- **Channel switching while loading.** Open channels or favourites while another channel connects.
  Fuse remembers your filters, scroll position and selected card when you return from playback.
- **More compatible IPTV playback.** Added support for playlist DRM information, including ClearKey,
  and improved handling of stream headers and playback cookies for sources that require them.
- **A more consistent Home order.** Personal rows come first, followed by Fuse or live channels,
  Streaming Networks and the remaining rows.
- **More resilient plugin loading.** Improved compatibility with CloudStream extensions and kept
  the interfaces they rely on available in optimised release builds.

## Fixed

- Fixed Continue Watching and Library pulling from a different tracking service than the one
  selected for the profile. Watchlists no longer silently fall back to Trakt.
- Fixed stale paused sessions bringing back titles or episodes after a newer completed,
  unwatched or removed action.
- Fixed Trakt watched history affecting Next Up and New Episodes when another sync service was selected.
- Fixed live-channel favourites failing to match the same channel across its different identifiers.
- Fixed supported IPTV sources losing required DRM or request information on the way to the player.
