# StreamDek TV v0.4.0

Emby joins Plex and Jellyfin, StreamDek can now reach for your own servers first when you press
Play, and Search tells you where every result comes from.

## What's New

**Emby on your TV**
Connect your own Emby server and its films and series join Home, Search and the media page, beside
Plex and Jellyfin. Servers on your network are found by themselves, or enter the address you use in
your browser. Sign in with your Emby username and password, or with **Emby Connect** to pick from
the servers linked to your emby.media account without typing an address with the remote. Your
passwords are used once and never kept.
- Add more than one Emby server, switch servers and libraries on or off, and choose whether Emby's
  in-progress titles also appear in StreamDek's Continue Watching.
- Your Emby setup follows your profile to your phone and the web portal.

**Prefer Media Server Source**
A new setting in **Settings > Streams and Quality**. Turn it on and, when you press Play without
choosing a source, StreamDek looks to your own Plex, Jellyfin and Emby servers first:
- **Direct Play** from your servers first, then **Direct Stream**, then the best source from your
  add-ons and plugins. A server copy that would need converting is kept for choosing by hand.
- A copy that plays well on this TV beats a bigger one that would not: a 1080p file that plays
  directly is picked over a 4K file that needs converting.
- The stream list follows the same order in every filter tab, so what plays automatically is the
  first source you see.
- A source you chose yourself, or asked StreamDek to remember, still comes first.

**Search, sorted by source**
Results now arrive in one section per place they were found: each of your Plex, Jellyfin and Emby
servers, StreamDek's catalogue, each add-on and each plugin. Every section is headed with its name,
what kind of source it is and how many results it found, and your own servers' sections carry the
service's mark so your copies stand out from across the room.
- Long sections show two rows to begin with. Move onto **Show all** beside the heading to see the
  rest, and **Show less** to fold them away again.
- A title found in several places is offered from each, so you can choose which one to play.
- The **Everything**, **Movies**, **Series** and **Live** filters still work, inside every section.

## Improvements

**Your servers' copies, everywhere**
A film or episode in StreamDek's catalogue now also lists the copies on your own servers among its
sources, and a title opened from your server lists your add-on sources too. Whichever you play,
your progress is saved to your server as well as to StreamDek.

**Continue Watching where you want it**
Choose for Plex, Jellyfin and Emby apart whether titles you are part-way through appear only on the
server's own page or also in StreamDek's Continue Watching.

**Resuming from your servers**
Resuming a title from Continue Watching now asks your server for a fresh link every time, so titles
that stalled on resume start playing again.

**The media page remembers where you were**
Coming back to the Plex, Jellyfin or Emby page from a title, another page or the menu now returns
you to the same row and card instead of the top of the page.

**Every version on a Plex server**
When a Plex server holds more than one version of a title, each is now offered, so a version that
plays directly on this TV is never hidden behind one that does not.

**Smarter Dolby Vision handling**
A Dolby Vision file that only plays correctly with a Dolby Vision decoder is no longer played
directly on a TV without one, so it does not play with the wrong colours. Plex sources now show
Dolby Vision, HDR10 or HLG, as Jellyfin and Emby already did.

**Cards that match their add-on**
Home rows from add-ons now show their cards in the shape the add-on asks for, square or landscape,
and fit logos and transparent artwork instead of cropping them.

**More accurate Plex and Jellyfin search**
Results from your own servers now only include titles that actually match what you searched for.
Plex also returns titles linked to a search through an actor, a genre or what it considers related,
and those used to appear as matches - searching "Euphoria" could list Tracker, Supergirl and
Hoppers. Matching ignores capitals, accents and punctuation, so "amelie" finds "Amélie" and
"spiderman" finds "Spider-Man", and one typo in a longer word is forgiven.

**Reaching a Plex server at home**
Some routers block the secure address Plex gives for your home network, which could leave a server
in the same room unreachable and make it look as if it could not be added. StreamDek now also tries
the server's plain local address on your home network when the secure one cannot be reached.

**Behind the scenes**
For StreamDek Ultra sources, StreamDek now notes whether playback started successfully, so a
provider that has stopped working can be spotted and fixed sooner. Server links are no longer kept
in the TV's local storage once a title has played.

## Quick Setup

- **Emby:** open **Settings > Emby**, pick your server from the list or enter its address, then sign
  in with your username and password or with **Emby Connect**.
- **Prefer Media Server Source:** open **Settings > Streams and Quality** and switch it on. It is off
  until you do, so playback works as before.
- **Continue Watching location:** open **Settings > Plex**, **Jellyfin** or **Emby** and choose where
  that server's in-progress titles appear.
- **Search:** nothing to set up. Move onto **Show all** beside a section's heading to see everything
  it found.
- **Plex at home:** nothing to set up. If your Plex server still cannot be reached, open its settings
  on the server and check that **Secure connections** is set to **Preferred** rather than
  **Required**.

## Good to Know

- Emby has no Quick Connect, so sign in with your password or Emby Connect. A server added through
  Emby Connect must be reachable from your TV.
- With **Prefer Media Server Source** on, StreamDek waits a few seconds at most for your servers when
  you press Play. A server that is slow or offline is skipped, and your add-ons and plugins play
  instead.
- A Dolby Vision file with no HDR10 layer is converted by your server on a TV without a Dolby Vision
  decoder, unless your player is set to MPV.
- A title in your Plex library that only matches your search by its cast or genre no longer appears
  under Plex in Search. Search by its title to find it.
- Plex servers set to **Required** secure connections will not accept the plain local address.
