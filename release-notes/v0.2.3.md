# StreamDek TV v0.2.3

## What's New

- Stream results now match the direct mobile pipeline more closely. Add-ons and plugins remain separate, all progressively returned results are retained, Usenet/NZB sources are supported, and compatible RAR-backed sources can reach playback.
- Cached streams are identified against the viewer's enabled premium services and show the cache provider, such as `Cached · Deepbrid` or `Cached · Real-Debrid`. Results are ranked intelligently with cached availability first and the preferred quality second.
- The TV player now includes an engine picker modelled on mobile, with available playback engines presented directly in the player.
- Cast members now open into a dedicated TV-designed details page with a circular portrait, readable and scrollable biography, and an aligned Known For collection.

## Improvements

- Stream sources arrive progressively and faster without waiting for a complete provider batch. Switching focus between source tabs now switches their content immediately, without requiring an extra click.
- The StreamDek result-formatting preference is available on TV and is honoured throughout stream parsing and presentation.
- Subtitle changes no longer unnecessarily stop or restart the active video, and player controls have been refined for TV focus behaviour.
- Episode synopses are overlaid compactly on episode artwork, watched seasons are marked on their season tabs, and More Like This now uses a coordinated shimmer-to-content transition instead of staggered card loading.
- Home content and artwork are prepared more smoothly to reduce piecemeal image loading. Profile selection now transitions the selected portrait into the centred loading state without briefly drawing it in the corner.
- Library and details transitions retain stable page geometry, and the shared navigation rail now stays collapsed during route handoff while remaining immediately focusable and navigable by the viewer.

## Fixes

- Fixed AIOStreams and other add-on results being truncated or lost as concurrent providers completed, including missing Deepbrid and NZB results.
- Fixed Remove from watchlist failing to persist, incorrectly showing an empty library, and not synchronising correctly with the supported watchlist providers.
- Fixed a Library crash after removing an item and pressing Down from the filters.
- Fixed the page shifting left before opening Library item details.
- Fixed title pages opening with the navigation rail expanded and the rail flashing open briefly after Back navigation.
- Fixed the navigation rail becoming unavailable after suppressing a transition-time focus flash.
- Fixed cast pages crashing when moving below a fully visible biography and made long biographies focusable and scrollable.
- Fixed the Home skeleton appearing behind the startup profile picker.
